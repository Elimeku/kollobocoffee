COMPREHENSIVE TRANSPARENCY

Our Sourcing Craftsmanship Timeline

Every single lot is systematically shepherded from high-altitude highlands to dry milling and container vacuum packaging, preserving flavor integrity.

01

Direct farm selection

We buy green coffee from smallholders, micro-lots and small washing station cooperatives in primary zones (Sidama, Guji, Yirgacheffe) at premium rates.

02

Strict Wet Milling

Fermentation times are strictly tracked. Sorting tables remove defective cherries immediately. Drying is closely monitored on raised African beds.

03

Q-Grading & Specimen Sheet

SCA cup profiles are verified before exporting. Moisture parameters must lock consistently between 10.5% - 11.5% with zero sensory flaws.

04

Cold-Chain Protection

Packaged in GrainPro bags and locked in insulated containers, avoiding moisture shock and volatile compound loss during sea shipping.
